# JobMatch logic tests: what practice providers publish (examples, item types, timing, difficulty)

Research date: 2026-10-26. None of the pages below show a publication date unless noted. Only JobMatch's own product page (jobmatchtalent.com) is official. Every sample item found comes from a test-prep provider's imitation; none are official JobMatch items. Image-based items (matrices, series) could not be read as images. Only the text explanations are recorded below.

## 1. What JobTestPrep shows for JobMatch (sections, counts, timing, sample items)

### Takeaway
JobTestPrep's Swedish JobMatch page describes LogiQ as a numeric section plus a logic section. Its logic items are picture series, visual associations, matrices and spatial/direction items, with language sometimes included. The simulation has 20 numeric and 20 logic questions. The page shows 5 free samples: 3 numeric (all text) and 2 figural. It gives no timing.

### Cited Findings
- JobTestPrep describes JobMatch LogiQ as a general logic test with a numeric part and a logic part. Numeric items vary in difficulty. Logic items include "bildserier, visuella associationer, matriser och spatiala frågor med riktningar", and language skills are sometimes tested too. The page shows no date apart from the footer copyright line "© 2026". — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
- The PrepPack's simulated JobMatch LogiQ test has **20 numeric + 20 logic questions**. The pack also contains numeric drills, logic drills (matrices, next-in-series), language drills and Swedish explanations. The page states **no time limit**. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
- Free sample items (JobTestPrep imitations), as given on the page:
  - **Numeric 1:** 700 − (−350) = ? Options: 350 / 450 / 700 / 950 / **1050** / 1350. Explanation: subtracting a negative number is the same as adding, so 700 + 350 = 1050. Check by computing 1050 − 350. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
  - **Numeric 2 (x→f(x)):** 4→12, 6→18, 8→24, 10→? Options: 26 / 28 / **30** / 32 / 34 / 36. The rule is ×3. The page's explanation is muddled: it also mentions +6 steps, which give the same answer. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
  - **Numeric 3 (number series):** 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36, ? Options: 42 / **45** / 48 / 50 / 52 / 54. The differences grow by 1 (+3 … +8, then +9). — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
  - **Logic 1 (3x3 matrix, image):** the answer is option 4. Across each row the figure changes and rotates 45°. Down each column the figure changes without rotating. Rows 1 and 3 contain the same figure. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
  - **Logic 2 (figure series, image):** the answer is option 2. Each frame holds a black circle, a white square, an X and an arrow. The square and the circle move one step clockwise and then pause. The X and the arrow pause and then move, in alternation. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
- JobMatch Talent is the separate Big Five personality test. JobTestPrep says its question count is similar to the simulated LogiQ test. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
- JobTestPrep's generic Swedish logic-test page gives timing guidance and examples for the following types:
  - **Syllogisms:** "Slutsatsen följer"-type items, 45–90 s each. Example: candidates must be "auktoritativa och/eller erfarna". 15 of 25 are known to be experienced. Conclusion: "Samtliga 25 kandidater kan vara erfarna". Answer: it follows, because of "kan vara".
  - **Scheduling puzzle:** Thomas, Sam, Ulla, Victor and Anna work Monday–Friday under constraints. The answer is "Thomas arbetar inte på onsdagar". About 90 s.
  - **Odd figure:** about 30 s. The odd one has a triangle corner that does not touch the outer rectangle.
  - **3x3 matrices:** 60–100 s each, 20–35 per test.
  - **Symbol series:** 60–90 s each, 8–35 per test. Rule: each arrow starts where the previous arrow pointed.
  - **Figure analogies:** about 1 min each, 8–20 per test. Rule: black and white swap, and the number of circles is kept.
  - **Set A / Set B / Neither:** 20–30 s each, 10–35 per test.
  - Advice: aim for about 8 of 10 correct in practice. Some tests have no penalty, so guess.
  — [JobTestPrep SE logiktest](https://www.jobtestprep.se/logiktest)
- The Danish page at jobtestprep.dk/jobmatch-test returned 404. No Danish or Norwegian JobMatch prep page turned up in search. — [jobtestprep.dk 404](https://www.jobtestprep.dk/jobmatch-test)

### Inferences
- The three numeric samples sit at an easy-to-moderate level, solvable in seconds. They line up with the app's mental arithmetic, x→f(x) and number-series types. Because JobTestPrep says the numeric items "vary in difficulty", the app should also include harder numeric items (two-operation functions, second-order series).
- The free samples include no syllogisms, odd-one-out words or seating puzzles for JobMatch specifically. Those types are documented only on JobTestPrep's generic logic page.

### Gaps
- JobTestPrep publishes no official timing, per-item time or item count for the real JobMatch test. 20+20 is the size of its simulation only.
- The figural answer options are images and could not be captured. No free PDF sample was found.
- The UK, DK and NO JobMatch pages were not found.

## 2. Official JobMatch LogiQ facts (vendor)

### Takeaway
On JobMatch's own description, LogiQ is adaptive and untimed, with time spent per item counting in the score. It takes 10–27 minutes, comes in 6 occupational versions and measures 5 areas, including "complex problem solving". The vendor publishes no sample items and no item count.

### Cited Findings
- LogiQ (JMLQ) is described as "ett modernt och innovativt adaptivt IQ och begåvningstest". Its tasks are language-independent, with instructions in 32 languages.
- It has **6 versions** by occupational area, from general practical jobs to advanced technology and natural science.
- It measures **5 areas**: numerisk förståelse, matematisk förståelse, allmän logik, avancerad logik and komplex problemlösning.
- It is **not time-limited**, but time spent per question counts in the scoring. It takes **10–27 minutes** depending on the version.
- Results are compared against norm groups. The page shows no sample questions and does not state the number of items.
— [JobMatch LogiQ product page](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/en/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/jobmatch-logiq/)
- The JobMatch homepage says LogiQ measures logical perception, logical thinking, mathematical ability, linguistic ability, and "the ability to find the common thread in complex contexts". — [jobmatchtalent.com](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/)
- Teamtailor's integration page (vendor marketing) mentions the six occupational levels and claims DNV validation for predictive validity. — [Teamtailor JobMatch integration](https://www.teamtailor.com/sv/integrations/jobmatch)

### Inferences
- An app could mirror the scoring by tracking response time per item, not only accuracy, and by offering an untimed mode that records time.

### Gaps
- No official source names a product called "JobMatch Logic" (as opposed to LogiQ). Searches for "JobMatch Logic" returned only LogiQ.
- **The claimed correlation with Mensa Danmark could not be verified.** No JobMatch page or other source found mentions Mensa. Several searches in Swedish and English came back empty.

## 3. Other providers and comparable Swedish tests (types, difficulty)

### Takeaway
The comparable Swedish tests (Matrigma, the Mensa-style tests, jobbcoach.ai) are mostly 3x3 figure matrices, plus verbal True/False/Cannot-say items and numerical table items. Matrigma's hardest items combine construction and frequency rules, sometimes along diagonals. That is likely harder than the JobMatch samples above.

### Cited Findings
- **Matrigma (Assessio):** Classic version has 35 items in 40 min, drawn from a bank and getting harder through the test. Adaptive version runs 12 min with up to 1 min per item, and gets harder after a correct answer. Items are 3x3 with one missing cell and 6 answer options, with no penalty for wrong answers. Rule families are motion, construction, frequency, rotation and progression. Early items use one rule. Later items combine rules, "some along the diagonals". These details come from a third-party prep site, not Assessio. — [practiceaptitudetests.com Matrigma](https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/testing-publishers/matrigma/)
- JobTestPrep's Matrigma page defines the rules. Progression is change in size, count or colour. Rotation. Frequency is the relationship between quantity, colour, direction, position and shape, which the page calls one of the trickier rules. Construction is A + B = C, where harder items have rules about which parts combine. Movement is inner objects moving against a fixed outer frame.
  The worked examples are:
  - (1) The white frame rotates clockwise while a black L rotates counter-clockwise and flips vertically.
  - (2) A cube "rolls" clockwise around an object.
  - (3) Construction combined with a frame type per row (dashed square, then circle, then rhombus).
  The score is a stanine-like 0–10 scale normed on the Swedish population: 0–3 is low (16%), 4–6 average (68%), 7–10 high (16%). The page suggests aiming for 7 or more for demanding roles. — [JobTestPrep SE Matrigma](https://www.jobtestprep.se/matrigma-test)
- Assessio's own feedback template says results are compared with a norm group, with high = 7–10. — [Assessio feedback attachment](https://support.assessio.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/360008588198)
- **jobbcoach.ai** has the following practice tests:
  - Matrislogik: 3x3 grid with options A–F, 15 items in about 20 min (basic) or about 25 min (advanced), with an expert level in Premium. The full mock runs 25 min.
  - Verbal: a short text plus a statement to judge true/false/cannot determine. 40 statements in about 20 min. The mock runs 40 min.
  - Numeriskt: tables and charts with percentage questions. 20 items in about 25 min.
  - Big Five personality.
  The site says it follows SHL, Cut-e and Assessio formats. It does not mention JobMatch. The only sample shown is a matrix with no stated answer. — [jobbcoach.ai rekryteringstester](https://www.jobbcoach.ai/verktyg/rekryteringstester)
- testtalent.se: the domain did not resolve on 2026-10-26, so it could not be checked.
- JobTestPrep's logic page lists other Swedish recruitment logic tests: Sova, Masters ACE, Cubiks/Talogy (used by Vattenfall, with symbol series), Alva Labs and Klarna (no penalty for wrong answers). — [JobTestPrep SE logiktest](https://www.jobtestprep.se/logiktest)

### Inferences
- To match Matrigma-level difficulty, the app's matrix generator should add multi-rule items: construction with selective overlay (XOR / AND of parts), frequency/distribution-of-three rules, and diagonal rules.
- Matrigma's pace is about 69 s per item (40 min / 35 items), consistent with JobTestPrep's 60–100 s guidance for matrices.

### Gaps
- No official Assessio Matrigma sample items were retrieved as text.
- The Mensa Sverige online test is not currently documented. A 2008 blog describes a Mensa Sweden and Expressen online test with a **10-minute limit** and "which figure is missing" items. — [jtbworld blog 2008](https://blog.jtbworld.com/2008/10/mensa-iq-test.html)

## 4. Mensa Danmark online test format

### Takeaway
According to secondary sources, the classic Mensa Danmark online test has 39 figural items in 40 minutes. Each item shows a grid of 6 figures with 1 missing, chosen from 8 options. No official mensa.dk page was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- 39 questions in 40 minutes. Each shows a grid of 6 shapes with 1 missing, and there are 8 options. The test is described as an older Flash-based version hosted by Mensa Denmark. — [TechJunkie](https://www.techjunkie.com/best-free-iq-tests-online/)
- 39 figure problems with a 40-minute limit, answered by clicking the fitting shape. — [karapaia.com](https://karapaia.com/?p=4504)
- A Serbian article on a similar Mensa-style test says there is no negative marking and all items carry equal weight, over 40 minutes. — [n1info.rs](https://n1info.rs/magazin/scitech/da-li-mozete-da-resite-mensin-test-proverite-da-li-spadate-u-2-najinteligentnijih/)
- The guessed URL mensa.dk/test-dig-selv/ returned 404. — [mensa.dk](https://mensa.dk/test-dig-selv/)
- Wikipedia says Mensa International itself offers no online IQ test, only the "Mensa Workout" quiz. — [Wikipedia: Self-test of intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-test_of_intelligence)

### Inferences
- The format is purely figural pattern completion at about 1 minute per item. If the Mensa correlation claim holds, LogiQ's "allmän/avancerad logik" parts would likely be matrix-like too. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No official Mensa DK page and no current test availability were confirmed (Flash is now dead). No source for the JobMatch–Mensa DK correlation was found.

## 5. LogiQ-like item types the app's list lacks

### Takeaway
Compared with the app's current list, the item types named by JobTestPrep, JobMatch and the comparable tests that are missing are:
1. Visual associations (figure analogies, A:B :: C:?)
2. Spatial direction items
3. Set A / Set B / Neither classification
4. Symbol series where each element starts where the previous one pointed
5. Rolling or orbiting-object matrices
6. Table- and chart-based numerical reasoning
7. Verbal True/False/Cannot-say from a passage
8. Vague "complex problem solving" (multi-step, multi-rule)

### Cited Findings
- JobMatch logic items named by JobTestPrep: picture series, **visual associations**, matrices, **spatial questions with directions**, and sometimes language. — [JobTestPrep SE JobMatch](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)
- JobMatch measures "komplex problemlösning" and "avancerad logik" alongside numerical and mathematical understanding. — [JobMatch LogiQ](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/en/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/jobmatch-logiq/)
- Figure analogies take about 1 min each. The example rule is a black/white swap with the circle count kept, and distractors differ in count or are identical to Z. Set A/B/Neither items take 20–30 s each; the example rule is no right angles plus at least one circle (A) versus an odd number of figures (B). — [JobTestPrep SE logiktest](https://www.jobtestprep.se/logiktest)
- Rolling cube and movement-against-frame rules appear in Matrigma prep. — [JobTestPrep SE Matrigma](https://www.jobtestprep.se/matrigma-test)
- Verbal T/F/Cannot-determine and numerical table/percentage items appear in jobbcoach.ai's SHL/Cut-e/Assessio-style practice. — [jobbcoach.ai](https://www.jobbcoach.ai/verktyg/rekryteringstester)

### Inferences
- Priority additions, in order:
  1. Figure analogies ("visual associations").
  2. Spatial direction items, for example "facing north, turn right twice, then left: which way?" or arrow and compass sequences. The exact JobMatch format is unknown.
  3. Adaptive difficulty with response-time scoring.
  4. Multi-rule matrices.
- The app's Sant/Falskt/Möjligt/Osannolikt syllogism format did not appear in any source found. JobTestPrep's syllogisms use "follows / does not follow", and jobbcoach.ai uses true/false/cannot determine.

### Gaps
- No real description of JobMatch "spatial direction" or "visual association" items exists beyond the category names. No candidate reports (for example on Flashback) turned up in search.
