# JobMatch Logic / LogiQ: first-hand candidate experiences

Research date: 2026-10-26. Overall: **first-hand candidate accounts are very scarce.** The public threads mostly contain candidates *asking* what the test looks like, not describing it. Most of the concrete detail comes from JobMatch's own documents, which describe the format and give examples. Those are vendor sources, not candidate experiences, and are labelled as such below.

## What do forums (Flashback, Familjeliv, Reddit, Glassdoor, Danish/Norwegian forums) say about JobMatch Logic/LogiQ?

### Takeaway
Only two forum threads name JobMatch LogiQ: Flashback t3642193 (Nov 2024) and Familjeliv thread 83084041 (Sept 2024, paywalled with HTTP 402). In both, candidates are asking before the test. Neither contains a post-test description of items, difficulty or score. No Reddit, Glassdoor, Quora or Danish/Norwegian candidate reports naming JobMatch were found.

### Cited Findings

They found no online information or sample questions for JobMatch LogiQ and asked whether it is the classic "vilken figur passar in" type. The role was a mid-level manager position. They asked whether the test has figures, number series, abstract reasoning or text-information tasks. Nobody answered with first-hand content. "PatricHbg" linked to the vendor's example page. "fonzen" replied in effect that an IQ test is an IQ test. Reliability: anecdotal, and it carries no information about the content. — [Flashback t3642193](https://www.flashback.org/t3642193)

  That LogiQ description repeats the vendor's wording, so it is probably copied, not experienced. Reliability: low. The text is unverified, comes only from search snippets, and appears to paraphrase the vendor. — [Familjeliv thread](https://www.familjeliv.se/forum/thread/83084041-iq-test-rekrytering-jobtalent-match-logiq-personlighetstest)
- **One search-engine summary claimed a Flashback user described the JobMatch logic test as "about 80 questions, with paper and pencil recommended".** I fetched the candidate Flashback threads (t3408842, t3485141 p2, t3028143, t1843772) and could not find that post. - **Prep vendor JobTestPrep (commercial, not a candidate source).** It describes LogiQ as numerical questions of varying difficulty plus picture series, visual associations, matrices, and spatial "direction" questions. Its own simulation has 20 numerical and 20 logic items. Its sample items include:
  - 700 − (−350) = 1050
  - a ×3 pattern
  - an increasing-difference series (answer 45)
  - a matrix where shapes rotate 45° across rows
  - a series where shapes step clockwise on alternating frames

  These are the prep company's own items, not recalled real items. — [JobTestPrep](https://www.jobtestprep.se/jobmatch-test)

### Inferences
- Candidates on Swedish forums treat LogiQ as an unknown quantity. The recurring complaint is that no sample questions exist online. This suggests low exposure and few post-test write-ups.
- LogiQ seems to be used for manager and mid-level roles. This is consistent with the Flashback OP and with JobTestPrep's claim that municipalities and companies use JobMatch for management positions.

## Which question types and difficulty do people remember (vs Mensa/Matrigma)? Number of questions, time, adaptivity, skip/back, instructions page

### Takeaway
No candidate recollections of specific items were found. The best concrete description of the item types and the score report comes from JobMatch's own documents. These describe two generations of the test:
- **Older "JobMatch Logic"** (report dated 2013; guide © 2018): 80 questions, 30-minute limit, a "Logikvärde" score of 80–160 correlated against Mensa Danmark, and seven sub-areas with example items.
- **Current "JobMatch LogiQ"** (launched about 2021+): adaptive, six job-level versions with 40 or 60 questions, roughly 10–27 minutes, and officially "not time-limited" but with time affecting the score.

### Cited Findings (vendor documents, high reliability for format, not candidate experience)
- **Older JobMatch Logic interpretation guide ("Tolkningsguide för användare och testtagare"):**
  - Format: "Testet har 80 frågor totalt och är tidsbegränsat till 30 minuter."
  - Scale: "Logikvärde: skala 80 – 160". The estimated population mean is 100–105, and the JobMatch database mean is 116, because test-takers are mostly white-collar applicants (managers, controllers, engineers, IT consultants).
  - Norming: "Testet är korrelerat mot Mensa Danmark".
  - Speed and accuracy: the report shows *Korrekt resonemang* (% correct of the items answered) and *Svarshastighet* (% of the 80 items reached). Their product is the total % correct. The JobMatch average is about 50% and the population average about 30%, which implies most people do not finish.
  - Skipping: the guide refers to people having "hoppat över frågor i denna kategori", which implies skipping was possible.
  - Example report: Logikvärde 121, Korrekt resonemang 81, Beslutshastighet 64, time 00:30, dated 2013-09-22.
  - The guide says the score can come out too low if the test is taken in a non-native language, if the person has dyslexia, if they were interrupted, or if they were nervous.

  — [JobMatch Logic sample report + guide (PDF)](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/JML-sample-sw.pdf). A 2021 version also exists at [JML_02_2021.pdf](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/JML_02_2021.pdf), which I did not read.
- **Item types in the older Logic test, with the vendor's own examples:**
  - *Logiskt tänkande*: syllogisms ("om sats A och sats B är sanna, är då sats C sann, falsk, möjlig, osannolik?") and odd-one-out words ("vatten, ånga, strand, is").
  - *Logisk uppfattning*: figures "som man ska vända och vrida på". The vendor says it does not measure 3D spatial ability, and averages are lower in this category.
  - *Matematisk förmåga*: calculation tasks and relations between numbers.
  - *Språklig förmåga*: word understanding, grammar and vocabulary. Averages are higher here.
  - *Komplex differentiering*: multi-step reasoning, e.g. "Om Lisa står till höger om Marko, som står till vänster om Erik, var står Sara?"

  These are the "direction/position" tasks. — [same PDF](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/JML-sample-sw.pdf)
- **Current LogiQ example-result page: six versions.**

  | Version | Questions | Avg. time | Avg. IQ | Subtests |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Advanced technology/science | 60 | 27 min | 116 | Math understanding, advanced logic, complex problem solving |
  | Qualified theoretical services | 60 | 25 min | 110 | — |
  | Leadership | 60 | 20 min | 105 | Numerical, general logic, complex problem solving |
  | Qualified practical | 60 | 25 min | 102 | — |
  | General theoretical | 40 | 10 min | 96 | — |
  | General practical | 40 | 10 min | 85 | — |

  — [JobMatch LogiQ example results](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/jobmatch-logiq-example-result/)
- **Vendor and partner descriptions:**
  - LogiQ is "adaptivt", "inte tidsbegränsat" (a unique algorithm factors in response time), "bara mellan 10 – 27 minuter", with results shown online and interactively against "olika normgrupper".
  - Its five domains are Numerisk förståelse, Matematisk förståelse, Allmän logik, Avancerad logik and Komplex problemlösning.
  - Instructions come in 31–32 languages, and the test is certified by DNV.

  — [Flashback t3642193 (quoting vendor)](https://www.flashback.org/t3642193); [Cision press release](https://news.cision.com/se/jobmatch-talent/r/jobmatch-sweden-lanserar-nyutvecklat-och-vetenskapligt-validerat-iq--och-begavningstest-med-sex-olik,c3572350); [Teamtailor integration](https://www.teamtailor.com/sv/integrations/jobmatch)
- **Comparison to Mensa:** the only link found is the vendor's own statement that the Logic score is correlated against Mensa Danmark. No candidate compared the difficulty to Mensa or Matrigma. — [PDF](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/JML-sample-sw.pdf)

### Inferences
- Candidates who took the test before about 2021 most likely saw the 80-item, 30-minute mixed test: verbal syllogisms, odd-one-out words, figure rotation, arithmetic and number relations, vocabulary/grammar, and position puzzles. It was strongly speeded, since the average candidate reaches only part of the test.
- Candidates since about 2021 more likely see adaptive LogiQ with 40–60 items, chosen by job level.
- "Feels like it gets harder" would be expected from the adaptive version, but no candidate confirms it.
- Whether going back is allowed in LogiQ is undocumented. Adaptive tests usually prevent it, but that is general knowledge, not a sourced fact.

### Gaps
- No first-hand recalled items, hardest-item descriptions, or candidate-reported LogiQ scores were found.
- No description of the candidate-facing instruction screen beyond the language count.
- I did not read the 2021 JML PDF. It may update the 80/30 format.
