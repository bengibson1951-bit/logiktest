# Unionen's own staff recruitment and its (possible) use of JobMatch tests

Research date: 2026-10-26. About 18 search/fetch calls. **Main result: no public evidence was found that Unionen uses JobMatch (Logic, LogiQ or Talent) for its own staff, and no public evidence of any named test vendor in Unionen recruitment.** Anything on which test or version a candidate gets is inference, not evidence.

## Do Unionen job ads mention JobMatch, logiktest, or tests in the process?

### Takeaway
None of the Unionen job ads checked (2025–2026, on jobb.unionen.se and Arbetsförmedlingen mirrors) mention JobMatch, logiktest, personality tests or any other test. The ads describe CV + motivation letter or screening questions, ongoing selection and interviews. Any test step is not announced publicly in the ad.

### Cited Findings
- Unionen's career site is jobb.unionen.se. On 2026-10-26 it listed two ads: "Enhetschef till Unionens regionala enhet" (Stockholm, deadline 2026-11-02) and "Ombudsman till Unionen Norrbotten" (Luleå, deadline 2026-10-26) — [jobb.unionen.se](https://jobb.unionen.se/)
- Ombudsman Norrbotten ad (2026): "Intervjuer kommer att ske löpande under ansökningstiden." It names teamchef Anders Rydehäll and a facklig kontakt. No tests, references or feedback step mentioned — [Ombudsman till Unionen Norrbotten](https://jobb.unionen.se/jobs/846-ombudsman-till-unionen-norrbotten)
- Enhetschef ad (2026): "När du söker tjänsten kommer du att få svara på ett antal frågor, vid sidan av din CV." and "Intervjuer sker efter sista ansökningsdag." No tests mentioned. Contact: Malin Engström, Kanslichef — [Enhetschef till Unionens regionala enhet](https://jobb.unionen.se/jobs/852-enhetschef-till-unionens-regionala-enhet)
- Fackliga rådgivare till Unionens förbundskontor (published 2025-05-08, deadline 2025-05-25, start 6 Oct 2025, so this is a cohort/intake hire): "Vi arbetar med löpande urval…", "I denna rekryteringsprocess kommer stor vikt att läggas vid personliga egenskaper." and "Motivationsbrevet ersätter det traditionella personliga brevet". No tests mentioned — [Jobbsafari mirror of Platsbanken ad](https://jobbsafari.se/jobb/fackliga-radgivare-till-unionens-forbundskontor-sasrb-19293949)
- Regional ombudsman Småland: per the search snippet, "Ansök med CV och motivationsbrev där du svarar på två frågor". The ad itself is now expired (404/"annonsen inte tillgänglig") — [jobb.unionen.se/jobs/742](https://jobb.unionen.se/jobs/742-regional-ombudsman-till-unionen-smaland)
- A further Fackliga rådgivare round for the Stockholm office was listed 2025-12-19, with no details retrieved — [vakanser.se](https://vakanser.se/hos/unionen/)
- The apply links use the URL parameters `rmpage=apply&rmjob=846` and `rm?rmpage=job&rmjob=830`. That is the ReachMee URL pattern, but the site does not name the vendor — [jobb.unionen.se ad 846](https://jobb.unionen.se/jobs/846-ombudsman-till-unionen-norrbotten); [jobb.unionen.se rm link](https://jobb.unionen.se/jobs/rm?rmpage=job&rmjob=830&rmlang=SE)
- The old career URLs unionen.se/om-unionen/jobba-hos-oss and /jobba-pa-unionen return 404. The current listing page is unionen.se/om-unionen/lediga-jobb, which links to jobb.unionen.se — [Lediga jobb hos Unionen](https://www.unionen.se/om-unionen/lediga-jobb)

It does not mention JobMatch. This page is advice to members, not Unionen's own hiring — [Unionen Jobbguiden: Intervju och tester](https://www.unionen.se/student/jobbguiden/intervju-och-tester)

### Inferences
- Unionen appears to run ReachMee (inferred from the `rmpage`/`rmjob` parameters). ReachMee supports third-party test integrations, but nothing found links Unionen's ReachMee setup to JobMatch.
- The ads stress "personliga egenskaper" and use structured screening questions. That is consistent with a later personality test step (JobMatch Talent or similar), but it is not evidence of one. If a test is used, it is probably sent to shortlisted candidates rather than announced in the ad, which is common practice in Sweden.
- If a candidate has been told by Unionen that they will take JobMatch tests, that invitation is the only reliable evidence. The public record is silent.

### Gaps
- Wayback/archived Unionen ads from 2022–2024 were not checked systematically.
- LinkedIn job posts were not checked because they need a login.
- No Unionen ad naming a test vendor or recruitment consultancy was found.

## Are there JobMatch customer references, case studies or press releases naming Unionen or other unions?

### Takeaway
No. JobMatch's public customer cases and testimonials name public agencies and companies (SCB, Skogsstyrelsen, Arvika kommun, WSP, Länsförsäkringar Västernorrland, Sjuhärads samordningsförbund), plus the transition organisations TRR and Omställningsfonden. None of Unionen, Vision, Akavia, Ledarna or Kommunal appeared.

### Cited Findings
- JobMatch's client testimonials page names SCB, Arvika kommun and Länsförsäkringar Västernorrland, and lists Trygghetsrådet TRR and Omställningsfonden as Swedish partners. It names no union — [JobMatch client testimonials](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/clients/client-testimonials/)
- Swedish customer cases name Sjuhärads samordningsförbund, Skogsstyrelsen and WSP — [Kundcase: "Vi är lyriska över JobMatch Talent"](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/kundcase/vi-ar-lyriska-over-jobmatch-talent/); [Kundcase: "Alla som gör JobMatch Talent känner sig stärkta"](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/kundcase/alla-som-gor-jobmatch-talent-kanner-sig-starkta/)
- JobMatch press releases on Cision cover international partnerships (e.g. Croatia 2024, Lithuania 2026 per the search summary). Searching "Unionen" + JobMatch on mb.cision.com returned only these releases, none naming Unionen — [Jobmatch Talent newsroom](https://news.cision.com/se/jobmatch-talent)
- JobMatch has a Teamtailor integration. Unionen does not appear to use Teamtailor — [Teamtailor JobMatch integration](https://www.teamtailor.com/sv/integrations/jobmatch)

### Inferences
- TRR and Omställningsfonden are social-partner transition bodies. Unionen is a party to the agreements behind TRR. This is a loose ecosystem link, not evidence that Unionen as an employer buys JobMatch.

### Gaps
- No full JobMatch customer list is public. The company claims about 4,500 active users without naming them (per the search summary of jobmatchtalent.com).
- Public procurement records showing Unionen buying test services were not found. Unionen is not a public authority, so it is not bound by LOU or ADDA framework agreements.

## Glassdoor / Indeed / forum interview reports for Unionen: what tests were used, how many questions and how much time?

### Takeaway
No first-hand candidate reports about tests in Unionen's hiring were found. Indeed has an Unionen employer page (3.6/5, 9 reviews) whose interview section could not be fetched (HTTP 403). Glassdoor searches returned no Unionen interview reviews.

### Cited Findings
- Indeed company page for Unionen: 3.6 stars from 9 reviews, with an "Intervjuprocess" section. The content was not retrievable (403) — [Indeed: Unionen](https://se.indeed.com/cmp/Unionen)
- Indeed listed a temporary student-member recruiter (phone/events) role and a public affairs manager role in Stockholm at search time — [Indeed: Unionen jobs](https://se.indeed.com/cmp/Unionen/jobs)
- A Glassdoor search for "Unionen interview" returned only other Stockholm employers (Universum, Axfood, SEB) — [Glassdoor search result example, not Unionen](https://www.glassdoor.ca/Interview/Axfood-Interview-Questions-E23346.htm)

### Inferences
- With no reports, the following comes from JobMatch product facts, not Unionen data. LogiQ is adaptive, has no fixed time limit, takes about 10–25 minutes depending on version, and response time affects the score — [JobMatch LogiQ product page](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/jobmatch-logiq/)
- Process shape: the JobMatch tools page describes the cognitive test as "often used with other recruitment tests". A common Swedish setup is Talent (personality) plus LogiQ sent together after a first screening or interview, with a feedback conversation ("återkoppling") on the Talent profile before or during the next interview. This describes general practice and vendor positioning, not Unionen-specific evidence — [JobMatch testing tools](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/); general Swedish practice per [Jobbland](https://jobbland.se/arbetsliv/klara-testet-nar-du-soker-jobb)

### Gaps
- The Indeed interview section and Glassdoor need logged-in or unblocked access. Flashback and Reddit were not found to contain Unionen-specific threads.
- The number of LogiQ questions is not published. It is adaptive, so the count can vary.

## What roles does Unionen typically recruit, and which LogiQ version would fit them?

### Takeaway
Unionen mostly recruits ombudsmän/förhandlare (regional), fackliga rådgivare (often in intake cohorts for the Stockholm förbundskontor) and managers (enhetschef/teamchef/kanslichef), plus some communications/public affairs and member-recruitment roles. For ombudsman, rådgivare, utredare and kommunikatör roles, the best-fitting LogiQ version is most likely **Qualified theoretical services**. For enhetschef/chef roles it is most likely **Leadership**. This mapping is my own inference: JobMatch does not publish a role-to-version table.

### Cited Findings
- Recent role types (2025–2026): Ombudsman Norrbotten, Enhetschef regional enhet, Fackliga rådgivare förbundskontor (cohort start Oct 2025), Regional ombudsman Småland, Ombudsman Gävle ("Arbetet består till stor del av förhandling") — [jobb.unionen.se](https://jobb.unionen.se/); [Jobbsafari](https://jobbsafari.se/jobb/fackliga-radgivare-till-unionens-forbundskontor-sasrb-19293949); [Upphandling24: Unionen Stockholm söker ombudsmän/förhandlare](https://upphandling24.se/karriar/platsannons/24093/)
- Also student-member recruiter (temporary) and public affairs manager — [Indeed: Unionen jobs](https://se.indeed.com/cmp/Unionen/jobs)
- LogiQ has six versions "from general practical roles to roles within advanced technology and science". It is an adaptive IQ/aptitude test measuring logical, numerical and verbal ability and the ability to find "den röda tråden" in complex contexts — [JobMatch LogiQ](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/jobmatch-logiq/); [JobMatch testing tools](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/)
- The JobMatch site uses both "JobMatch Logic" and "JobMatch LogiQ" for the cognitive test. Logic appears to be the older or alternate name, and LogiQ is the current adaptive product — [JobMatch testing tools](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/)

### Inferences
- Likely version per role (inference):
  - Ombudsman / förhandlare (labour law, negotiation, academic level typical): **Qualified theoretical services**
  - Facklig rådgivare (phone/digital advice on labour law and agreements): **Qualified theoretical services**, or possibly **General theoretical services** if the employer sets a lower bar for an intake role
  - Utredare / analytiker / kommunikatör / public affairs: **Qualified theoretical services**
  - Enhetschef / teamchef / kanslichef: **Leadership**
  - Student-member recruiter / admin / service: **General theoretical services**
  - Advanced technology and science and both practical versions are unlikely for Unionen roles.
- Most likely single answer for a typical Unionen interview candidate (ombudsman or rådgivare): **JobMatch LogiQ, Qualified theoretical services**, usually alongside JobMatch Talent. This is unconfirmed.
- Whether a candidate gets Logic or LogiQ: LogiQ is the current product, so a 2025–2026 process would most likely use LogiQ. The invitation email or link from JobMatch normally states the test name.

### Gaps
- JobMatch does not publicly list which roles each LogiQ version targets, its norm groups, or question counts.
- No confirmation that Unionen uses JobMatch at all. To confirm, check the test invitation email (sender domain jobmatchtalent.com or JobMatch Centre) or ask the Unionen contact named in the ad.
