# What JobMatch Sweden AB publishes about JobMatch Logic and JobMatch LogiQ

Research date: 2026-10-26. Sources are jobmatchtalent.com (live and Wayback Machine captures), the public LogiQ example reports on myjobmatchcenter.com (an Angular app, read in a real browser), four LogiQ research PDFs hosted on jobmatchtalent.com, and Cision press releases. Each source carries its date where one is known.

Naming note: the research papers use "JML" and "JobMatch Logic Aptitude test" for the instrument now sold as **LogiQ (JMLQ)**. Their scales (Complex, Math, Numerical, Logical, Speed) match LogiQ. They do **not** match the old commercial "JobMatch Logic" / Logikanalys, which reported Logikvärde, språklig förmåga and the other sub-areas listed below. Treat "JML" in the papers as the LogiQ prototype, not the old 30-minute test.

## Q1. Are there sample or example questions anywhere on jobmatchtalent.com?

### Takeaway
I found no sample items for either test on any JobMatch page, PDF or result example. JobMatch publishes result examples (reports), not questions. The only item-format facts come from the research papers and the example reports:
- LogiQ items are scored right/wrong.
- LogiQ items are described as language-independent.
- The Speed scales use spatial items, and Speed2 mixes numerical and spatial items.
- Each subtest has 20 items.

### Cited Findings
- The current Swedish LogiQ product page has no example questions. Its only image is an illustration, "jobmatch_illustration_01". — [JobMatch LogiQ (sv)](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/)
- The Wayback Machine has captured the following JobMatch URLs. All of them are result examples or guides; none is an item bank or practice test:
  - LogiQ page attachments, captured 2022: `/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/logic_m_preview_edited/`, `/logiktest/`, `/logiktest-resultatexempel/`
  - PDFs: `/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/logiktest.pdf` and `/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf`
  - JobMatch Talent (personality test) material: `/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Tolkningsguide.pdf` and `/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/JobMatch-147074-Tolkningsguide.pdf`
  - Source: [Wayback CDX listing of jobmatchtalent.com](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=jobmatchtalent.com/*&collapse=urlkey&fl=timestamp,original)
- `logiktest.pdf` (still live, HTTP 200) and `Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf` are both "LOGIKANALYS" result reports for the old JobMatch Logic, not question booklets. Their text layer has only the report page. Each PDF says it has 79 pages, but no question text could be extracted. — [logiktest.pdf](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/logiktest.pdf); [Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf (Wayback 2022-10-04)](https://web.archive.org/web/20221004221902/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf)
- LogiQ: "Uppgifterna i testet är språkoberoende och instruktioner finns på 32 språk" (the tasks are language-independent, with instructions in 32 languages). The current test-tools overview says 31 languages. — [LogiQ page, Wayback 2022-05-25](https://web.archive.org/web/20220525135213/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/); [Testverktyg overview](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/)
- Item scoring: "The items for each JMLQ factor were binary (true/false)." This most likely means scored correct/incorrect, not a True/False answer format; the paper does not describe the response format. — [Jansson, Erixon & Archer 2022, J Anxiety & Depression 4(2), DOI 10.46527/2582-3264.147](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-scientific-study-JAD-4.2-2582-3264.pdf)
- The Speed scales use spatial items: "Speed2: … a mix of Numerical and spatial items (whereas Speed only consists of spatial items)." — same source
- In the research version, the "additional scale, labeled as speed, was created with a subset of items from the complex and logical scales." — [Jansson, Erixon & Archer 2022, Curr Res Psychol Behav Sci 3:1065](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-scientific-article-CRPBS-V3-22-1065.pdf)
- Scale definitions in the papers:
  - Complex cognition: "ability to understand complex ideas and information"
  - Mathematical understanding: "general understanding of mathematics principles"
  - Numeric understanding: "general understanding of numbers based on basic arithmetic"
  - Logical reasoning: "ability to make inference-based conclusions"
  - Sources: the CRPBS and JAD 4.2 papers above
- Subtest descriptions shown in the live reports:
  - Numerical understanding: "Shows your skills in basic arithmetic"
  - General logic: "Demonstrates your ability to draw logical conclusions"
  - Mathematics: "Shows your skills in more advanced mathematics"
  - Advanced logic: "Shows your ability to draw advanced logical conclusions"
  - Complex problem solving: "Demonstrates your ability to grasp complex contexts"
  - Source: [LogiQ example reports, linked from the example-result page](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/en/jobmatch-logiq-example-result/)

### Inferences
- The likely item mix for a practice app:
  - Numerical: arithmetic.
  - Mathematics: harder maths principles.
  - Logic: inference, almost certainly figural or symbolic, since the tasks are language-independent.
  - Complex problem solving: grasping complex relationships.
  - Spatial items: used at least in the research Speed scales.
- Because the items are language-independent, verbal analogies and reading-comprehension items are unlikely in LogiQ. The old JobMatch Logic did have a "språklig förmåga" (linguistic ability) sub-score.

### Gaps
- No public LogiQ or Logic item, screenshot of an item, or answer-option layout. The number of answer options is not stated anywhere I found.
- No candidate instruction page ("instruktioner för testtagare") or LogiQ FAQ was found on the site or in Wayback.
- The 2022 attachment pages `logic_m_preview_edited` and `logiktest` are image-attachment pages. I did not render the images, so they may show a report preview; unverified.

## Q2. What do the LogiQ example result pages show?

### Takeaway
Each live example report shows the following:
- A total score on an IQ-type scale, with markers at mean ±1 and ±2 SD (15-point steps). The axis is centred on the selected occupational norm group's mean.
- One scaled score per subtest.
- A norm-group selector with the six groups.
- A statistics table per subtest: items answered, items correct, and the candidate's time next to a reference time.
- Each subtest has exactly 20 items.
- Candidates can leave items unanswered (one example shows "55 of 60" answered).

### Inferences
- The score uses the IQ metric (M=100, SD=15). The axis ticks sit at mean −2SD … +2SD of the selected occupational norm group: 96 = the v5 group mean (listed as 96), and 116 = the v1 group mean (listed as 116).
- **Speed matters.** A candidate with 83% correct but well over the reference time scored only 97 (v5). In v1, the same 83% correct scored 114 against a group mean of 116.
- The second time column ("10:00", "27:00", and so on) is most likely the norm-group average or a reference time per subtest. Its values differ slightly between reports of the same version: v2 shows 24:45 against the advertised 25 min, and v6 shows 13:40 against the advertised 10 min. This suggests it is not a fixed time limit.
- Unanswered items ("15 of 20" answered) suggest either a skip/next function or a per-section cutoff. JobMatch does not say which.
- A practice app should:
  - use 20-item subtests;
  - record time per item;
  - score with a rate-based formula (correct answers per unit of time);
  - show both accuracy and time against a reference time.

### Gaps
- Whether the reference time is a limit, an average, or a target is not explained anywhere.
- No per-item response times are shown in the reports.
- The full PDF export of the report was not opened.

## Q3. How do adaptivity, timing and scoring work, and what norms are used?

### Takeaway
JobMatch calls LogiQ "adaptivt", but publishes no adaptive algorithm. I found no description of IRT, item selection or stopping rules. The published research describes rate-based scoring (correct answers per unit of time), standardized to IQ (M=100, SD=15). The "adaptive" label may refer to choosing one of the six versions, not to computerized adaptive testing; this is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- "JobMatch LogiQ (JMLQ) är ett modernt och innovativt adaptivt IQ och begåvningstest… JobMatch LogiQ är inte tidsbegränsat – i stället används en unik poängsättningsmetod där tiden som testtagaren lägger på att besvara frågorna tas med i poängsättningsprocessen… tar … bara mellan 10 – 20 minuter." In English: LogiQ is a modern, innovative adaptive IQ and aptitude test. It is not time-limited; instead, a scoring method includes the time the candidate spends answering. It takes only 10–20 minutes. — [LogiQ page, Wayback 2022-05-25](https://web.archive.org/web/20220525135213/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/)
- The current page (2026) says 10–27 minutes depending on version, and that results are interactive and comparable to different norm groups. It still gives no detail on the adaptive mechanism. — [LogiQ page (sv), live](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/)
- Rate-based scoring: "Rate, defined as correct answers per unit of time, was established as a consistent measure… all standardized test scores for the JMLQ, were based upon the rate unit." The same paper says "Response times had served as the time limits related to each specific scale." — [CRPBS 3:1065, published 2022-10-10](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-scientific-article-CRPBS-V3-22-1065.pdf)
- Norming in the CRPBS paper:
  - IQ distributions were built with M=100, SD=15.
  - The norm group was initially N=1028; after removing outliers, General N=1017, Numeric 1004, Math 1001, Logic 1005, Logic2 998, Complex 990, Speed 1013.
  - The sample was 73.1% women, recruited via LinkedIn and Facebook.
  - The General score is the mean of the trait z-scores, excluding Speed.
  - There are six occupational sub-norm groups: advanced tasks, leadership, administration, practical skills, communication, practical work.
  - Source: same CRPBS paper
- Percentiles of the General IQ in the norm group: P5 72.6, P10 79.5, P25 90.2, P50 100.8, P75 110.5, P90 119.3, P95 123.8. — same CRPBS paper, Table 1
- Item counts in the research version (CFA on 77 items) and Cronbach's alpha:

| Scale | Items | Alpha |
|---|---|---|
| Complex | 18 | .75 |
| Mathematical | 14 | .88 |
| Numerical | 20 | .83 |
| Logical | 25 | .72 |
| Speed | 11 | .56 |
| Speed2 | 12 | .66 |

  The model is second-order, with a General factor. — [JAD 4(2), Aug 2022](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-scientific-study-JAD-4.2-2582-3264.pdf)
- An earlier research version had **84 items** in five categories (Complex, Mathematical, Numerical, Logical, Speed) and N=1028. Correct answers and time-to-answer correlated −0.60 to −0.89 per item, except mathematical items at −0.27. — [Archer, Jansson, Olsen & Erixon 2021, Int J Sch Cogn Psychol 8(1):215](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-Scientific-article-IJSCP2012JML.pdf)
- The education/occupation study reports scales "General, Speed and Speed2" and the Basic scales "Complex, Mathematical, Numerical, and Logical". Mean proportion correct ran from low to high in this order: Complex, Math, Logic, Numeric. Complex was the hardest and Numeric the easiest. — [JAD 5(2), published 2022-12-23, DOI 10.46527/2582-3264.151](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JobMatch-LogiQ-scientific-study-JAD-5.2-3264.151.pdf)
- LogiQ is listed as DNV-certified on the current test-tools page. — [Testverktyg](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/). JobMatch's Cision releases attribute DNV certification explicitly to JobMatch Talent, the personality test. — [Cision PDF 2024](https://mb.cision.com/Main/11841/4114694/3302596.pdf)

### Inferences
- The commercial version has 20 items per subtest × 2 or 3 subtests. That fits a selection from the research item pool of roughly 77–84 items, plus the advanced variants.
- Expected order of difficulty for calibration, easiest to hardest: Numerical < Logic < Math < Complex.

### Gaps
- No public description of the adaptive algorithm.
- No public statement on whether candidates can go back to earlier items.
- No public description of the instruction or practice screen a candidate sees.
- The occupational sub-norm sizes in the commercial version are not published.

## Q4. When did LogiQ launch, is JobMatch Logic discontinued, and is "logikdelen" likely LogiQ?

### Takeaway
The old JobMatch Logic (Logikanalys) was sold until at least mid-2021. LogiQ replaced it on the website by May 2022. As of 2026, only LogiQ is listed, so a current JobMatch invitation's logic part is very likely LogiQ. I found no launch press release.

### Cited Findings
- Old JobMatch Logic, as described on jobmatchtalent.com in captures from 2015 to 2021:
  - "Testet används som ett komplement till JobMatch Talent för att verifiera personens logiska förmåga… logisk uppfattning, logiskt tänkande, matematisk förmåga, språklig förmåga, samt förmågan att finna den 'röda tråden' i komplexa sammanhang. Testet är tidsbegränsat till 30 minuter och görs online via webben."
  - In English: the test complements JobMatch Talent to verify logical ability in several ways — logical perception, logical thinking, mathematical ability, linguistic ability, and finding the common thread in complex contexts. It is limited to 30 minutes and taken online.
  - The pages also say "På flera olika språk" (in several languages).
  - Sources: [Wayback 2015-12-09](https://web.archive.org/web/20151209140357/http://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/om-jobmatch/guide-till-resultaten/logik-och-begavningstest/); [Wayback 2021-06-21](https://web.archive.org/web/20210621105312/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/logik-och-begavningstest/)
- The English page in May 2021 read "JobMatch Logic is a logic, aptitude and IQ-test… The test is limited to 30 minutes and is taken online." Its free-trial form offered "JobMatch Talent / JobMatch Logic / JobMatch Screen". — [Wayback 2021-05-17](https://web.archive.org/web/20210517100148/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/en/testing-solutions/your-testing-tools/logic-and-aptitude-test/)
- Old report format ("LOGIKANALYS"):
  - Header fields: Profil, Testtagare, "Tid 00:30:0:0", Datum, Test ID, Projekt.
  - Description: "Logik Analysen ger en bild av hur snabbt en person kan identifiera en fråga eller ett problem och komma fram till svaret eller lösningen." (The analysis shows how quickly a person can identify a question or problem and reach the answer or solution.)
  - It reports **Logikvärde**: 104 in the 2012 example and 134 in the 2014 example.
  - It also reports percentage bars:
    - Korrekt resonemang (correct reasoning)
    - Beslutshastighet (decision speed)
    - Inlärningsförmåga (learning ability)
    - Logiskt tänkande (logical thinking)
    - Logisk uppfattning (logical perception)
    - Matematisk förmåga (mathematical ability)
    - Språklig förmåga (linguistic ability)
    - Komplex differentiering (complex differentiation)
  - Example values for the HR-strateg profile (2014): 89 / 77 / 68 / 95 / 20 / 50 / 95 / 40 %.
  - Sources: [logiktest.pdf, ©2013](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/logiktest.pdf); [Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf, ©2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20221004221902/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Logiktest-resultatexempel.pdf)
- By May 2022 the same Swedish test-tools slot is a "JobMatch LogiQ" page, and the trial form lists "JobMatch Logiq" instead of Logic. — [Wayback 2022-05-25](https://web.archive.org/web/20220525135213/https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/jobmatch-logiq/)
- The 2022 papers call LogiQ "the recently introduced recruitment instrument". The research data (N=1028) predates the 2021 IJSCP paper. — CRPBS 2022 and IJSCP 2021 (above)
- The 2026 test-tools page lists JobMatch Talent, Career, Screen, LogiQ and Lingua SE. There is no "JobMatch Logic". — [Testverktyg (live)](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/sv/testlosningar/testverktyg/)
- Partners in Croatia (2024) and Hungary (December 2025) package "Jobmatch Talent and Jobmatch LogiQ" together. — [Cision 2024](https://mb.cision.com/Main/11841/4114694/3302596.pdf); [Cision 2025](https://mb.cision.com/Main/11841/4369856/4176446.pdf)
- A legacy GDPR page "integritetsavtal-jobmatch-logic" existed from 2019 to 2021. Its Danish counterpart, "fortrolighedsaftale-jobmatch-logic", was still captured in 2024. A LogiQ-specific GDPR page exists in English. — [Wayback CDX](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=jobmatchtalent.com/*&collapse=urlkey&fl=timestamp,original); [LogiQ GDPR (EN)](https://www.jobmatchtalent.com/about-us/personal-data-agreement/for-candidates-who-take-our-tests/information-about-the-processing-of-personal-data-jobmatch-logiq/)

### Inferences
- LogiQ replaced JobMatch Logic between mid-2021 and May 2022. A JobMatch invitation in 2026 that includes a logic part is very likely LogiQ: 40 or 60 items, 20 per subtest, no hard overall time limit but speed counts, and language-independent.
- The old Logic is not offered on the website. Legacy customers might still have access in JobMatch Center; unverified.
- The old Logic had a hard 30-minute limit and a linguistic sub-score. LogiQ has no linguistic subtest and does not advertise a hard limit.

### Gaps
- **"80 questions" and the "Logikvärde 80–160" range were NOT found in any JobMatch source.** The archived pages confirm only 30 minutes and the sub-areas; the reports show Logikvärde values of 104 and 134. These two specifics need a non-JobMatch source.
- No dated LogiQ launch press release was found on Cision or on the JobMatch news pages.
- I did not check the LinkedIn or YouTube posts, or the jobmatchtalent "videor" page, for item demonstrations.
- I did not find the DNV certificate document for LogiQ itself.
